About EmergencyConnect

EmergencyConneet is a university-developed, student-led demonstration prototype. Our mission is to explore and showcase innovative digital solutions for streamlining critical communication during medical emergencies. This platform aims to create a cohesive network, bridging emergency requesters, ambulance dispatchers, and hospitals to explore response time reductions and resource visibility. It is designed to be a tool for learning and future-focused research in the emergeney services sector.
